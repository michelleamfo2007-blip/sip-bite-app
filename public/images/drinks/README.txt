Place your drink images in this folder with these exact filenames:

- coca-cola-300ml.png
- fanta-300ml.png
- sprite-300ml.png
- water-500ml.png

These will be served at:
- /images/drinks/coca-cola-300ml.png
- /images/drinks/fanta-300ml.png
- /images/drinks/sprite-300ml.png
- /images/drinks/water-500ml.png

After adding, refresh http://localhost:5174/menu to see them.
